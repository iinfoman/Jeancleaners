Your Safety Matters

Solo Cleaner. Safe Systems.

Jeanwyne works alone — which means safety protocols protect both of you. Here's how.

🪪

ID Verified & Badged

Jeanwyne carries a verified ID badge at all times. You can request to see her ID before any clean begins. No ID shown = no entry.

📍

Live Check-In System

Jeanwyne checks in via WhatsApp to a trusted contact on every job arrival and checks out on departure. If no check-out is received, a welfare check is triggered.

📸

Before & After Photos

Jeanwyne photographs the space on arrival and completion. This protects you from false damage claims and protects Jeanwyne from false accusations.

📋

Booking Records

Every booking is logged with your name, address and contact number. Records are kept securely. No anonymous jobs accepted.

🚫

Right to Decline

Jeanwyne reserves the right to decline or leave any job where she feels unsafe. Clients who are aggressive or inappropriate are blacklisted immediately.

📞

Emergency Contact

A trusted emergency contact has Jeanwyne's schedule at all times. In any incident, they can provide job location and client details to authorities.

💛

A note from Jeanwyne: "I take your safety and my safety seriously. These systems aren't just policies — they're how I do every single job. You deserve to trust who is in your home, and I deserve to feel safe doing my work."

Questions

Good to Know

Do I need to supply cleaning products?▾
Yes — please have your own cleaning equipment and supplies ready. Jeanwyne brings the skill and elbow grease; if you'd like her to bring supplies too, just mention it when booking and she'll quote accordingly.
Can I book a recurring clean?▾
Absolutely. Choose weekly, bi-weekly or monthly on the booking form and Jeanwyne will set up a regular schedule with you — plus recurring clients get priority booking slots.
What if I need to cancel or reschedule?▾
Just WhatsApp Jeanwyne as early as possible. Please give at least 12 hours' notice where you can, so the slot can be offered to someone else.
Is my personal information safe?▾
Yes. Booking details are only used to schedule and deliver your clean, kept securely, and never sold or shared with third parties — in line with POPIA.
How do I pay?▾
Payment is on completion, once you've had a chance to walk through the space with Jeanwyne. EFT and cash are both accepted.
